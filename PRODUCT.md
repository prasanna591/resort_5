# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (user's choice, confirmed 2026-11-23).

## Users

Affluent leisure travellers planning an alpine escape: couples, families, and small groups who book a high-end hotel stay directly. They arrive comparing a handful of luxury properties and need to understand the rooms, the food, the wellness offering, the setting, and the feeling of the place before committing.

## Product Purpose

A marketing and direct-booking site for an invented boutique five-star alpine hotel. Success means a visitor understands the property's character, finds a room and season that fits, and uses the booking enquiry form with confidence.

## Positioning

A small, design-led mountain house with a strong sense of place: an original brand built around quiet alpine ritual — light, stone, wood, water, and long-table dining — rather than generic resort opulence.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse on desktop and mobile, often in several sittings, before enquiring. The site must work as a standalone, static-friendly Next.js application with no backend dependency for its primary content. All rooms, rates, and availability shown are illustrative and must be labelled as such.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page, scroll-driven hotel showcase with anchored sections and a booking enquiry form.
- No real reservations engine, payment, inventory, or availability data exists; any dates/prices shown are illustrative.
- Imagery must be original, licensed stock or generated assets — nothing copied from an existing hotel's site.
- Deployable as a standard Next.js app.

## Brand Commitments

Invented boutique brand; user asked for an original five-star Alpine hotel identity with fictional name, story, rooms, and copy. No real hotel name, trademark, or existing brand assets may be used.

## Evidence on Hand

No real photography, copy, testimonials, pricing, or press. All narrative content is authored for this build and is explicitly illustrative. No third-party claims may be presented as fact.

## Product Principles

- The sense of place comes first: the mountain, the light, and the materiality of the house.
- Specificity over superlatives: concrete rooms, dishes, and rituals instead of empty luxury language.
- The visitor always knows where they are on the page and what to do next.
- Every visual and word is original to this project.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-reachable navigation and form controls, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts from 390px wide upward.
